How to play

  • Tap a cell to mark an X, double‑tap to place a panda.
  • Swipe across cells to X several at once.
  • Wrong spot = a sad panda and one bamboo lost (3 per level).

What to look at

  • Splash → loading → tutorial on first launch.
  • Home: profile avatars & frames, streak, settings.
  • Win screen, daily streak, out‑of‑bamboo screen.

Notes

  • Progress is saved in your browser, per theme.
  • Settings → “Reset progress” replays the tutorial.
  • Best experienced on a phone.
